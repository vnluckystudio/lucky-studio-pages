A little garden of puzzles

Each board is a small color-and-layer puzzle. The right caterpillar can clear a path to a different color underneath, so take a moment to look over the leaves before choosing your next cup. As the chapters unfold, new colors and natural leaf shapes add fresh possibilities.

How to play

  1. Pick a cup
    Choose a cup of caterpillars from the tray and place or drag it into an open seat.
  2. Match the leaves
    Caterpillars travel to exposed leaves of their color and nibble them away, opening the layers below.
  3. Clear the board
    Plan the next color, uncover every layer, and watch the caterpillars complete their transformation.
